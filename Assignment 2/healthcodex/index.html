Health CodeX Workshop 2023

About the Workshop

Health CodeX is a one-day workshop on health messaging and the coding of disease classification. It brings together clinicians, health information managers, students and software developers.

Venue

  • Location: Colombo Theatre, UNSW Sydney
  • Address: High Street, Kensington NSW 2052, Australia
  • Phone: +61 2 1234 5678
  • Facilities: Wheelchair access, free Wi-Fi, catering, parking nearby

Call for Abstracts

Professionals and students are invited to submit an abstract for a talk or poster.

  • Maximum 300 words
  • Include title, authors and affiliation
  • Topic must relate to health coding or messaging standards
  • Submit by email as a PDF or Word file

Email your abstract to organisers@healthcodex.example .

Submission deadline: 15 July 2023
Notification of acceptance: 1 August 2023
